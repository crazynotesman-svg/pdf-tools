Many everyday documents arrive as separate PDFs: contracts, invoices, or
application papers. Instead of sending them one by one, it often makes sense to
combine them into a single file.

## Why merge PDFs?

- **Order:** All documents for one matter live in a single file.
- **Easier sharing:** One email, one attachment, fewer chances for error.
- **Privacy:** With local tools, the file never leaves your browser.

## How it works

1. Select the PDF files you want to combine.
2. Set the page order.
3. Merge the files and download the result.

Our tool does all of this entirely in your browser – with no upload to a server.
That keeps sensitive documents private.
