When scanning, pages easily end up upside down or in landscape. The good news is
that a PDF can be rotated without trouble.

## When to rotate PDFs

- A single page was scanned the wrong way up.
- The whole document is in the wrong orientation.
- Certain pages need aligning for printing.

## Steps to rotate

1. Load the PDF into the tool.
2. Select the pages you want to rotate.
3. Pick a direction (90°, 180° or 270°).
4. Save the rotated document.

Because everything runs locally in your browser, your files stay on your device.
