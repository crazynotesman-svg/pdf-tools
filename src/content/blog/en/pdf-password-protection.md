Contracts, proposals, certificates, business documents: some PDFs are too important to send around unprotected. The people who should open them must be able to – but not everyone who happens to get hold of the file should see its contents. A password is the simplest way to achieve that.

## Open password vs. permissions password

PDF protection comes in two levels, and it's worth telling them apart:

**Open password:** Without the password, the file cannot even be opened. Anyone with the password sees the full content and can work with it.

**Permissions password:** The file opens without a password, but certain actions are restricted – printing, copying, or editing, for example.

For most personal and business cases, the open password is enough: it prevents unauthorized people from accessing the content at all. That's exactly what the [Protect PDF](https://pdf-tools-7q5.pages.dev/en/pdf-protect/) tool does.

## Password protect PDF locally in your browser

The most important point for your document's security: encryption happens directly on your device. The file is **not** uploaded to a server. That means:

- Your document never leaves your computer.
- There is no copy on a third-party server that could be viewed or deleted later.
- You don't have to trust any service – the protection is created locally, right in front of you.

With a local tool, you password protect a PDF right in the browser – no detours, no upload.

## How to protect a PDF in three steps

1. **Select a file:** Drag and drop your PDF into the tool or pick it from the file dialog.
2. **Set a password:** Choose the password that will be required to open the file. Pick one you can remember – there is no recovery.
3. **Download:** Save the protected file to your device. From now on, the PDF asks for the password when opened.

## What to keep in mind

An honest note: a lost password cannot be recovered. Passwords are neither stored nor transmitted – storing them would be a security risk. Keep the password somewhere safe, for example in a password manager.

Good to know: when a password is added, the pages are embedded into the new file as high-quality images. The visual content stays fully intact – but text selection is no longer possible afterwards. For documents that only need to be read and distributed securely, that's usually no drawback.

## When is password protection worth it?

- **Sending confidential documents:** proposals, pay slips, contract drafts.
- **Archiving sensitive records:** the file stays protected even on shared devices.
- **Time-limited access:** you decide who gets the password when – and can change it later.

Want to remove the protection later? Use the [Unlock PDF](https://pdf-tools-7q5.pages.dev/en/pdf-unlock/) tool – provided you know the password. A complementary measure for documents that get passed around is adding a [watermark](https://pdf-tools-7q5.pages.dev/en/pdf-watermark/), for example with the recipient's name.

## Privacy and GDPR

For privacy-conscious users it matters that local PDF protection transfers no personal data: no cookies, no logs about your files, no server access. It's a simple privacy solution – nothing is transmitted, nothing is processed on third-party systems.
