Sometimes you need a PDF page as an image – for a slide deck or a social post.
Converting to JPG is straightforward.

## Why PDF to JPG?

- Images embed anywhere.
- No PDF reader needed to view the content.
- Individual pages can be exported on demand.

## How to convert

1. Select the PDF.
2. Choose which pages to export.
3. Start the conversion.
4. Download the JPG files.

The process runs entirely locally, so your documents are never uploaded.
