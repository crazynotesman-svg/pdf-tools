A watermark identifies a document at a glance: "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", "COPY", or your company name in subtle gray across every page. It makes it immediately clear who created the document and how it should be treated – and it makes unintended redistribution harder.

## What watermarks are for

Watermarks are more than decoration. Typical use cases:

- **Marking confidentiality:** "CONFIDENTIAL" or "INTERNAL" tells the recipient how to handle the document.
- **Showing status:** "DRAFT" prevents misunderstandings when a document isn't final yet.
- **Tracing distribution:** A watermark with the recipient's name reveals where a document came from if it ends up online.
- **Branding:** Your company name or logo in the background reinforces the brand with every export.

## Choosing opacity, position and size well

A good watermark informs rather than interferes. Three settings make the difference:

- **Opacity:** At 10 to 25 percent, the content stays perfectly readable while the watermark remains clearly visible. For urgent notices like "CONFIDENTIAL", a higher opacity is fine.
- **Position:** A diagonal layout across the whole page is the most visible and hardest to remove. A corner or a centered stamp works too.
- **Size:** Large enough not to be missed, small enough not to cover the text.

With the [PDF Watermark](https://pdf.100ideas.net/en/pdf-watermark/) tool you set the text, opacity and size – the distribution across pages happens automatically and stays local in your browser.

## How to add a watermark

1. **Select a file:** Drag and drop your PDF into the tool or pick it from the file dialog.
2. **Set text and style:** Type the text you want, e.g. "CONFIDENTIAL", and adjust opacity and size.
3. **Download:** Save the watermarked file to your device.

The process takes only a few seconds – on desktop and mobile alike.

## Frequently asked questions

### What text can I use as a watermark?

Anything you like: "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", "COPY", your company name, or the recipient's name.

### Does the watermark cover my content?

No. The watermark is laid semi-transparently across the pages diagonally, so the content stays readable. You control the opacity.

### Can I remove the watermark later?

An added text watermark becomes part of the page and cannot be easily removed. Keep a copy of the original if needed.

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The watermark is applied entirely locally in your browser. Your file never leaves your device.

### Is the tool free?

Yes. Adding watermarks is free forever and requires no registration.

### Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Because processing runs in the browser, it works on desktop and mobile alike.
